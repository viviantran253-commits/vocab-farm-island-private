Level `L` requires `50 + 25 × (L − 1)` additional XP to reach the next level. No predefined level cap. Harbour orders grant 20% bonus gold but ordinary XP for all crops sold.

There are 10 total plots. Current plot unlock requirements and one-time prices are listed in the table below.

An emergency carrot seed is available only when the player has less than 8 gold, no seeds, no harvested crops and no crops growing.
